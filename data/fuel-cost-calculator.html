    Sources & method

    Prices for the week of {{ fc_week }}, read on {{ fp.updated | date: "%-d %B %Y" }}. Method checked {{ page.content_checked | date: "%-d %B %Y" }}.

    Prices
    European Commission, Weekly Oil Bulletin, price developments 2005 onwards: Euro-super 95 petrol and automotive gas oil (road diesel), the consumer price with taxes that each country reports as most often charged. Published in euros per 1,000 litres and shown here per litre. The same figures as on petrol and diesel prices in Europe.
    EU average
    The Commission’s own figure, weighted by how much of each fuel each country uses.
    {%- if uk %}
    UK prices
    Department for Energy Security and Net Zero, Weekly road fuel prices: the average UK pump price of ultra-low-sulphur unleaded petrol and diesel, with duty and VAT, for the Monday of each week. Published in pence a litre.
    Pounds and euros
    The European Central Bank’s euro reference rate for the pound on the Monday of the UK prices’ week, or the last working day before it: £{{ uk.gbp_per_eur }} to the euro for {{ uk_week }}. The same rate converts both ways.
    {%- endif %}
    Calculation
    Litres = distance in km × litres per 100 km ÷ 100, and cost = litres × price, for each country on the route, rounded to the cent only at the end. A mile is 1.609344 km. A UK gallon is 4.54609 litres, so litres per 100 km = 282.481 ÷ mpg.
    Update schedule and reuse

    The prices are refreshed by a scheduled download each week, after the Commission publishes. A failed download leaves the previous week in place, and the date at the top of the page says which week you are reading. This is not a live feed.{% if uk %} UK prices more than two weeks older than the EU’s are taken off the page rather than set against another week.{% endif %}
