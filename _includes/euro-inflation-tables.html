Recovery means the first sampled month after that trough at or above the previous peak, on the selected nominal or real path. A recovery in euros can precede recovery after inflation. These are asset returns without top-ups: new contributions can lift a balance while the asset remains below its high. Monthly sampling can miss deeper falls within a month; it does not show that every intra-month fall was deeper. Gold uses monthly averages.
