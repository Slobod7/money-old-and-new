Sources & method

Figures to {{ ib_month }}, as Eurostat published them on {{ ib.source_updated | date: "%-d %B %Y" }}, read on {{ ib.updated | date: "%-d %B %Y" }}. Explanatory notes checked {{ page.content_checked | date: "%-d %B %Y" }}.

Inflation
Eurostat, harmonised index of consumer prices (HICP), annual rate of change: the index in a month against the same month a year earlier, in percent, as Eurostat rounds it to one decimal. Monthly, not seasonally adjusted, from {{ ib_since }}.
Core
The same rate for all items except energy, food, alcohol and tobacco. It starts later than the headline rate: in {{ ib.core_from.EA | append: "-01" | date: "%B %Y" }} for the euro area, and by {{ ib.core_by | append: "-01" | date: "%B %Y" }} in every country.
Euro area
Eurostat’s figure for the area as it was each month, with a country counted from the month it adopted the euro. It is weighted by household spending, so it is not an average of the bars on this page.
Ranking
This site’s ordering of Eurostat’s rates for the latest month all {{ ib_countries }} countries have. Countries with the same rate are listed by name.
Gap
This site’s subtraction: a country’s published rate minus the euro area’s, in percentage points.

Each update compares every figure with the copy in the ECB Data Portal, which this site’s ECB rates chart reads. At the last update, {{ ib.ecb_check.differing }} of {{ ib.ecb_check.compared }} differed, none by more than 0.1 point. A larger difference, a missing month or a country that stops reporting leaves the page as it was.

Update schedule and reuse

The saved data is refreshed by a scheduled download on the 18th of each month, with manual updates possible. The page ends at the last month every country has reported, so a flash estimate for the euro area alone does not move it, and a full release that comes out after the download shows at the next one. Eurostat revises earlier months, and an update takes the revisions with it. This is not a live feed.

Eurostat allows reuse of its data with the source named. The ranking, the gap to the euro area and each country’s highest rate are this site’s work on those figures.
