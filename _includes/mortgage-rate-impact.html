A cheaper home, a bigger payment?

Change the loan size, rates and fall in the amount borrowed. These are illustrative bank rates, not a forecast of ECB decisions.

A €200,000 loan over 25 years costs €800 a month at 1.5%. At 4%, even a 15% smaller loan costs €897: about 12% more each month.

Assumptions and calculation

Both loans are fully repaid with equal monthly payments over the same term. Annual rates are nominal loan rates divided by 12. Payment = P × m ÷ [1 − (1 + m)−n], where P is the principal, m the monthly rate and n the number of months. At zero interest, payment = P ÷ n.

The smaller loan is a scenario, not evidence of an observed house-price fall. A fall in the house price only reduces the loan by the same percentage if the loan-to-value ratio stays the same. Deposits, income, fees, insurance, taxes and future rate resets are outside this comparison.
