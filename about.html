A Sunday letter on macro, for people in Europe

{{ site.title }} is a free letter for people in Europe who want to know what the ECB, inflation and markets mean for their money. It covers old money (cash, deposits, bonds, gold, stocks) and new money (Bitcoin, stablecoins), with no price calls and no tips on what to buy or sell.

I’m Bogdan, and I write it from Bucharest. Today I’m a content writer at Bitcoin.com, and I own some Bitcoin. Before that I was senior finance copywriter at Satoshi Club, where I owned everything the outlet published across X, Telegram, Medium and YouTube, and senior copywriter and editor at Coinweb.

More than 500 finance and crypto articles so far.
