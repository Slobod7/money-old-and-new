How this scenario works

Tax is charged only on positive interest. The balance fee is then taken from the principal plus after-tax interest. We find the lowest non-negative gross rate that leaves at least the starting purchasing power after your assumed inflation. No tax refund or negative interest is modelled.

The one-year balance factor is (1 + gross rate × (1 − tax rate)) × (1 − fee rate). Dividing it by (1 + inflation) gives its purchasing power relative to the starting balance. All rates in this formula are decimals.
