Give each chart on a page its own id. Away from /data/inflation-by-country/, the source caption ends with a link to that page, and the comment before the figure lets that page find the articles that use the chart (_includes/data-articles.html). Jekyll draws it from _data/inflation_by_country.json: the country against the euro area, headline inflation, the range asked for, as inline SVG. So it reads the same without JavaScript, and the table beneath has every month of both measures. assets/js/inflation-by-country.js, added once per page below, adds the choice of country, of what to compare it with, of measure and of range, and a readout on hover, tap and the arrow keys, from assets/data/inflation-by-country.json.
