What would your euros be worth today?

Money that sits still loses value. Since the euro began in {{ since.from | append: "-01" | date: "%B %Y" }}, prices in the euro area have risen {{ since.prices_up }}%, so €1,000 kept in a drawer since then buys what €{{ since.drawer }} did.

Making your money work for you means beating that, without taking risks you can’t live with. Every option below has had its bad years: stocks that halved, German bonds that lost two-fifths of their value after inflation from 2019, and Bitcoin, which has fallen by three-quarters more than once. What matters is knowing why each one moves, and holding a mix you won’t panic out of.
