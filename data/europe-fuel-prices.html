    Sources & method

    Prices for the week of {{ f_week }}, read on {{ fp.updated | date: "%-d %B %Y" }}. Explanatory notes checked {{ page.content_checked | date: "%-d %B %Y" }}.

    Prices
    European Commission, Weekly Oil Bulletin, price developments 2005 onwards: Euro-super 95 petrol and automotive gas oil (road diesel), the consumer price each country reports as most often charged, with and without taxes. Published in euros per 1,000 litres and shown here per litre.
    EU average
    The Commission’s own figure in the same bulletin, weighted by how much of each fuel each country uses. It is not an average of the {{ fp.countries }} figures on this page.
    Tax
    This site’s subtraction: the price with taxes minus the price without, from the same row of the bulletin. The split of a four-week change into its two parts is the same subtraction at both dates.
    Map
    Outlines made with Natural Earth, simplified, on an equal-area projection. Overseas territories and the smallest islands are left out of the drawing.
    Dates, currencies and gaps

    The bulletin dates each row by a Monday; countries collect their prices on different days around it. Outside the euro, the Commission converts national prices to euros, so a currency move changes the price shown. The four-week change compares the latest week with the bulletin’s row four Mondays earlier. A country with no figure for a week is shown without one, never filled in.

    Update schedule and reuse

    The saved data is refreshed by a scheduled download each week, after the Commission publishes, with manual updates possible. A failed download or check leaves the previous week in place, and the date at the top of the page says which week you are reading. The Commission can revise earlier weeks. This is not a live feed.

    The Commission allows reuse of its content with credit and with changes indicated: here, the conversion to euros per litre, the tax subtraction and the rounding are this site’s. Natural Earth’s map data is in the public domain.
