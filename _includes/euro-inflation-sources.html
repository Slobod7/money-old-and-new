Behind the numbers

Sources & method

Primary data, the timing exceptions and the calculation rules — all in one place.

Explanatory sources checked 2 November 2026. The update date applies to the combined dataset; separate provider retrieval dates are unavailable. Source revisions can change earlier results.

Inflation & euro conversion
Inflation and euro conversion
Eurostat via ECB: HICP.M.U2.N.000000.4D0.INX, monthly, not seasonally adjusted, 2025 = 100. Index ratios, not annual inflation rates, convert amounts. HICP observations available to {{ df.hicp_to | append: "-01" | date: "%B %Y" }}; the calculation stops at your end month, by default the latest month all sources share. Dollar assets are converted using the last available ECB daily USD-per-EUR rate each month, EXR.D.USD.EUR.SP00.A; gold uses EXR.M.USD.EUR.SP00.A, the monthly average. Dollar values are divided by dollars per euro. These are unhedged euro returns.
Cash, deposits & the synthetic Bund
Cash and deposit proxy
Cash earns zero nominal interest. The deposit uses ECB MIR.M.U2.B.L22.F.R.A.2250.EUR.N: household new-business deposits with agreed maturity up to one year, from {{ df.from.deposit | append: "-01" | date: "%B %Y" }}. Each month compounds by (1 + annual rate/100)^(1/12), using that month’s rate. This is a reinvestment proxy, not the return on a particular fixed-term account; it assumes monthly reinvestment at the changing average rate. ECB marks 2000–02 rates as estimates.{% if df.deposit_filled.size > 0 %} Missing latest rate observations carried forward from the preceding available month: {% for m in df.deposit_filled %}{{ m | append: "-01" | date: "%B %Y" }}{% unless forloop.last %}, {% endunless %}{% endfor %}.{% else %} No deposit rate is carried forward in this data vintage.{% endif %}
Synthetic German Bund
ECB IRS.M.DE.L.L40.CI.0000.EUR.N.Z, Germany’s monthly-average 10-year government-bond yield. A hypothetical bond bought at par, with annual coupon equal to the previous yield, is repriced one month later at the new yield with 9 years 11 months remaining, then replaced by a new 10-year bond. Coupons and principal are discounted at that yield; monthly returns are compounded. This is a synthetic constant-maturity path, not an observed bond fund or a bond held to redemption. Monthly-average yield inputs are not executable month-end prices.
US & European stock returns
US stocks
Kenneth R. French, F-F_Research_Data_Factors: monthly Mkt−RF plus RF reconstructs the dollar market return, with dividends reinvested, then converts to euros. This is a value-weighted portfolio of eligible US-incorporated CRSP ordinary shares on NYSE, AMEX and Nasdaq. It is neither the S&P 500 nor literally every listed US company. The library changed its US input format in January 2025 from FIZ (month-end dividend reinvestment) to CIZ (daily compounding and ex-date reinvestment). Rebuilt histories can therefore differ. No fund tracking error, investor withholding tax or dealing costs are modelled.
European stocks
Kenneth R. French, Europe_3_Factors: monthly Mkt−RF plus RF, in dollars, with dividends and capital gains, converted to euros. The developed-Europe portfolio includes the UK and Switzerland. These are research market returns, not a specific ETF’s investor return.
Gold & Bitcoin prices
Gold
World Bank Pink Sheet, Gold, Monthly Prices, US dollars per troy ounce, divided by monthly-average USD/EUR. Purchases and valuations use that month’s averages, not month-end closes. The series models price exposure, with no interest or dividends; spreads, storage and insurance are omitted.
Bitcoin
Coin Metrics PriceUSD, daily dollar closing price, using the final available daily observation in each month, converted at the ECB’s last available daily rate. History begins {{ df.from.bitcoin | append: "-01" | date: "%B %Y" }}. The price and foreign-exchange fixes need not be simultaneous. Trading, custody and network costs are omitted.
Purchases, returns & inflation adjustment
Purchases and returns
A lump sum enters at the selected month’s valuation; monthly contributions include both the start month and endpoint month. Each purchase uses that month’s series level, with the gold and Bund timing qualifications above. A lump sum’s annual return is its compound annual growth rate; monthly buying uses a money-weighted annual rate (XIRR, actual days/365). For less than a year the widget shows the total change. Fees, taxes and product costs are excluded from all historical results.
End-month euros
Inflation-adjusted figures are in the prices of your end month, by default {{ cc_to }}, not the current day. Each historical balance is multiplied by end-month HICP / that month’s HICP. Each contribution is converted separately into endpoint-price euros. At the endpoint this factor is 1, so the same final euro balance appears with either inflation setting. The inflation-adjusted contribution total and returns change; the endpoint is not deflated a second time.
HICP coverage & the one-year deposit scenario

Eurostat’s HICP metadata explains the changing-composition euro-area aggregate and national weighting. Its coverage notes describe the basket and exclusions, including imputed rent.
